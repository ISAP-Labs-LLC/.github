# ISAP Labs

**Information Systems Advanced Protection.** We build practical network software for homes and small businesses.

### Helios
A network monitor for your home. One screen shows every device, your connection to the internet, your firewall, your DNS filter and your gear, and tells you when something breaks. Free for personal use; Helios Mobile is $8 a year.

▶ [Watch the 4-minute tour](https://youtu.be/cKyXhrx8xqc)

### Argus · in development
Network security with an AI that runs on your own hardware. Argus reads your intrusion detection system's alerts, decides what really matters, explains why in plain English, and blocks threats with a one-tap undo. It has watched our own network around the clock since November 2026.

▶ [Meet Argus in 2 minutes](https://youtu.be/06fz9xqBLuc)
